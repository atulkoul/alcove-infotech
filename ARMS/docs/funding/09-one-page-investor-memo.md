# AlcoveHire One-Page Investor Memo

## Company

AlcoveHire (by Alcove Infotech) is an AI-powered recruitment SaaS platform that helps agencies and hiring teams reduce time-to-hire, improve conversion rates, and gain pipeline visibility.

## Problem

Recruitment teams commonly operate across fragmented tools, spreadsheets, email, and chat. This creates slow shortlist cycles, poor stage tracking, and weak forecast accuracy. Existing ATS products are often either too expensive or too complex for small and mid-sized firms.

## Solution

AlcoveHire combines ATS workflow and AI actions in one platform:
- Candidate and requirement pipeline management
- AI-powered parsing, scoring, and profile summaries
- Interview tracking and follow-up automation
- Billing and revenue visibility in one dashboard

## Why Now

- Recruitment digitization is accelerating across SMB and mid-market teams.
- AI acceptance in daily workflows has increased significantly.
- Teams need cost-efficient software with direct productivity impact.

## Business Model

- SaaS subscription with monthly and yearly plans
- AI-credit add-ons for usage expansion
- Additional expansion revenue via seats and premium support

Current plan points:
- Starter: INR 2,999 per month
- Growth: INR 7,999 per month
- Business: INR 18,999 per month

## Go-To-Market

- Phase 1: founder-led sales to first 20 accounts
- Phase 2: outbound, webinars, and referrals to reach first 100 customers
- ICP: staffing agencies and in-house hiring teams with 5 to 50 recruiters

## 12-Month Targets

- Product launch readiness in 4 months
- 100 paying customers by month 12
- MRR at 100 customers: about INR 6.1L
- ARR at 100 customers: about INR 73.2L

## Team Plan

Build and launch team:
- Product: 1 PM, 2 frontend developers, 2 backend developers, 1 QA, 1 DevOps/Security, 1 UI/UX
- GTM: 1 sales lead, 2 sales executives, 1 customer success, 1 marketer

## Funding Ask

Seeking INR 2.2 Cr for a 12-month runway.

Use of funds:
- Product and engineering: 52%
- Sales and marketing: 28%
- Cloud and operations: 8%
- Legal and compliance: 2%
- Contingency reserve: 10%

## Why This Can Win

AlcoveHire is positioned at the intersection of operational depth and price-to-value fit for underserved recruitment teams. With a focused 4-month build timeline, disciplined hiring, and outcome-oriented GTM, the company can reach early revenue milestones and become fundable for the next growth stage.
