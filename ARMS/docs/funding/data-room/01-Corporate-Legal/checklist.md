# 01 Corporate and Legal Checklist

- Certificate of Incorporation
- PAN, GST registration
- MOA and AOA
- Shareholding pattern and cap table
- ESOP plan and board approvals
- Founders agreement
- Advisor and consultant agreements
- Material contracts and vendor agreements
- Standard customer MSA, order form, and SLA template
- Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
- Refund and cancellation policy
- Data Processing Addendum template
- Ongoing legal disputes declaration (if any)
