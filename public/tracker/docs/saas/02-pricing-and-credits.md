- Billing cycles: Monthly and Yearly

Rules:
- If an action fails due to system error, auto-refund credits.
- If user cancels before completion, do not charge.
- All deductions must be traceable in credit ledger.

## 4) Fair Usage and Overage Rules

- Hard limit on plan resources (users/candidates/requirements) unless upgraded.
- AI overage allowed when add-on packs are enabled.
- Overage billing unit: 1000-credit pack.
- At 80 percent and 95 percent credit usage, send alerts.
- At 100 percent usage without add-on, disable only AI features (core ATS remains usable).

## 5) Free-to-Paid Conversion Rules

- Trial duration: 14 days from workspace creation.
- Trial end behavior:
  - If payment added: convert immediately to selected paid plan.
  - If no payment: move to read-only mode for 14 additional days.
  - After grace period: suspend access until subscription starts.

## 6) Upgrade, Downgrade, and Cancellation

- Upgrade: immediate, prorated charge.
- Downgrade: effective next billing cycle; no mid-cycle data deletion.
- Cancellation: remains active until period end.
- Reactivation: restore prior tenant data for up to 90 days after cancellation.

## 7) Recommended Public Pricing Copy

- "Start free for 14 days. No long-term contract."
- "AI credits included in every paid plan."
- "Save 20 percent with yearly billing."
- "Scale seats and AI credits as your team grows."

## 8) Packaging Suggestions for Sales

- Starter target: small agencies and early-stage HR teams.
- Growth target: scaling recruitment firms with multiple recruiters.
- Business target: large agencies and in-house TA with high volume.
- Enterprise target: custom security, SSO, SLA, and onboarding.
