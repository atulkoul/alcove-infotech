Who we are

Technology-Powered Recruitment. Human Expertise.

Alcove Infotech is a modern recruitment and staffing company helping organizations identify, engage, and hire quality talent.

Our recruitment approach combines:

AI & Technology + Multi-Channel Sourcing + Human Expertise

We leverage AI-enabled tools to search and analyze talent across multiple sources, identify relevant professionals, support candidate matching, and build targeted talent pipelines.

Our recruiters then validate candidate suitability through human interaction, experience assessment, communication, and role-specific evaluation.
