About Alcove Infotech

A smarter approach to recruitment.

We combine modern recruitment technology with experienced human recruiters to create a faster, more targeted hiring process.

Our philosophy

Technology finds. People decide.

AI can improve how talent is discovered and evaluated, but successful hiring still requires human understanding.

Our approach brings together artificial intelligence for sourcing, profile analysis, candidate matching, and talent mapping; recruitment expertise for conversations, validation, and decision-making; and industry knowledge for the realities of each function.

Our vision and mission

To become a trusted, technology-driven talent acquisition partner enabling smarter hiring decisions through AI and human expertise.

Discover better

Use intelligent tools and multi-channel sourcing to reach relevant professionals.

Evaluate deeply

Keep human-led conversations and role-specific assessment at the heart of selection.

Partner longer

Build transparent, quality-driven relationships with clients and candidates.

What we value

Integrity, quality, innovation, human connection, commitment, and partnership.

  • Transparent and ethical interactions with clients and candidates
  • Relevant, qualified talent over unnecessary volume
  • Continuous exploration of technology to improve recruitment
  • Ownership of requirements from brief through joining

We don't just search resumes. We discover talent.
